Fizz technology helps manage bookings, customer information, payments, reminders and attendance. The **Fizz Kidz
Portal** is the best place to start and should show the information you need.

<Aside type="tip" title="Start with the Portal">
  Treat the Portal as your source of truth. Start there for bookings, customer information, attendance, and reports.
</Aside>

## What the Portal helps with

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Bookings and enrolments" icon="notes">
    View parties, programs, dates and customer details.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Customer communication" icon="email">
    Send confirmations, forms, reminders and important links.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Payments and invoices" icon="document">
    See the payment and invoice information relevant to each booking, and take payments at the studio on its Square
    Terminal.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Attendance" icon="approve-check-circle">
    View session lists, allergies, pickup details and attendance.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Reports and planning" icon="analytics">
    Review capacity, upcoming bookings and inventory requirements.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Team tools" icon="setting">
    Access onboarding, payroll, discount codes and other Fizz tools.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## Other tools you may see

You may come across these names while using Fizz technology:

| Tool                | What Fizz Kidz uses it for                                      |
| ------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Acuity**          | Program sessions and appointment scheduling                     |
| **Square**          | Payments, invoices, refunds, gift cards and the studio terminal |
| **Sling**           | Rosters and shift information                                   |
| **Google Calendar** | Confirmed parties and events                                    |
| **Zoho**            | Customer follow-up and communication                            |

You do not need to understand how these tools connect. Start in the Portal and follow the relevant guide when a task
needs another tool.

## If something looks wrong

Record:

- the customer or booking;
- the studio and date;
- what you expected to see;
- what the Portal shows; and
- a screenshot of any error.

Report the problem to the person who manages Portal support.
