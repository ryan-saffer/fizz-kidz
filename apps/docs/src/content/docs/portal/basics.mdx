The [Fizz Kidz Portal](https://bookings.fizzkidz.com.au) is the main staff workspace for bookings, programs, attendance,
creations, payments at the studio, reports, inventory, and selected administration tasks.

## Sign in and get oriented

<Steps>
  <ol>
    <li>
      Open the <a href="https://bookings.fizzkidz.com.au">Portal</a>.
    </li>
    <li>
      Sign in with Google or your email and password. Use <strong>Forgot your password?</strong> if needed.
    </li>
    <li>Open the menu at the top left to move between tools.</li>
    <li>Check the studio shown at the top right before changing studio-specific information.</li>
  </ol>
</Steps>

The Portal remembers the studio you last selected on that browser. If you have access to more than one, use the studio
switcher to change context.

<Aside type="note" title="Your Portal may look different">
  Tools are shown according to the studio and responsibilities attached to your account. Ask the person who manages
  Portal access if your work requires an administration, report, inventory, or editing option you cannot see.
</Aside>

## The studio iPad

Each studio's iPad signs in to the Portal with its own studio account. It shows that studio's bookings, and it's where
staff take payments on the studio's Square Terminal: [Collect party payment](/services/birthday-parties/collect-payment/)
for parties and [Sell products](/portal/pos/) for our kits.

## Know which page you are using

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Staff dashboard" icon="setting">
    Signed-in pages for day-to-day work. The studio switcher and navigation menu stay at the top.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Public customer page" icon="information">
    Booking, enrolment, form, and invitation journeys can open without the staff dashboard. Share only the intended
    customer link, not a staff dashboard URL.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## Before changing anything

- Confirm the studio and date.
- Open the correct child, customer, booking, or class.
- Read allergy, anaphylaxis, additional-information, and status tags where shown.
- Use the page's **Save**, **Create**, **Sign in**, or **Generate** action to complete the change.
