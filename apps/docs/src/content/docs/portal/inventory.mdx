The master organisation can choose any studio. A studio organisation sees its own stock. Managers, admins and the studio iPad can receive,
count and remove stock. Only super admins can create or edit items.

## On hand, reserved and available

For cakes and take-home bags, each row shows three numbers:

- **Here**: everything physically at the studio, including stock reserved for upcoming parties.
- **Reserved**: held for parties that have ordered it. Reserved stock stays in the freezer until the party.
- **Available to order**: here minus reserved. Only this can be ordered on the party form.

When a party is paid on the studio's Square Terminal, its reserved stock comes off automatically. When a booking is deleted, its reservation is given
back automatically.

<Aside type="tip" title="Counting the freezer is safe">
  Reserved cakes aren't labelled, and you don't need to know which is which. When you count, count everything in the
  freezer. The count should match **Here**.
</Aside>

    <li>

      at zero.
    </li>
    <li>

    </li>
  </ol>
</Steps>

<Aside type="caution" title="Receive what arrived, never what's in the freezer">
  Enter the delivery from the docket, not by counting the freezer afterwards. Some of what is already there is reserved
  for upcoming parties.
</Aside>

## Count and remove

- **Count stock**: a stocktake of every cake, cake supply and bag at once. Count everything, including reserved stock, and leave blank anything you didn't count. **Count** on a single row does the same for one item.
- **Count**: enter what is physically there. For cakes and bags, a count that doesn't match needs a reason, and the
  studio is emailed. If the count leaves fewer than are reserved, some parties will be short, and the studio is told
  so they can sort it out.
- **Remove (thrown out)**: in the **…** menu. Use it when something spoils or is damaged, with a reason.
- **Stock history**: in the **…** menu. Every receive, count, removal, reservation and party, with who did it and why.

For party food, use **Receive** when stock arrives and **Count** after a stocktake. Level-based items use **Update
level**. **Mark count unknown** is for when a supply's count can no longer be trusted, and **Mark unused here** is for
when a studio doesn't use an item. Don't mark an item unused when it is only out of stock.

## Running low

Each item can have a running-low number. When stock drops to it, the row shows **Running low** and the studio's email
address is emailed once so they can reorder. For cakes and bags, running low is measured against what's still available
to order. Count mismatches and parties that will be short go to the same address. Kym is copied on every alert, and the
Customer team (bookings@) on Geelong's.

## Setting up items

Only super admins create and edit items. Every cake, cake supply and take-home bag is linked to Square:

- **Cakes and take-home bags** are linked to the cake design or bag in **Ordered by customers as**. That's how the party
  form shows what's available and reserves it. Each design or bag can only be linked to one item.
- **Cake supplies** are linked to the serving or candle option that uses them in **Used by**. Cups and spoons can both
  use "Ice-cream cup with spoon". **Used per party** says how much comes off: one per party (candles) or 1.5 per child
  (cups, spoons). At Werribee and Geelong, a paid serving or candle option is only offered when a supply is linked to it,
  so options the studio doesn't stock (like waffle cones) aren't shown.

Linked items start at zero at Werribee and Geelong, ready for the first delivery, and are marked unused at other
studios.
