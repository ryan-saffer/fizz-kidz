Inventory shows what each studio has on hand. It holds two kinds of item on one page:

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Cakes & take-home bags" icon="star">
    Werribee and Geelong sell these from studio stock. Parents order them on the party form, and each order reserves
    stock for that party.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Party food" icon="document">
    Food the studio uses at parties. Staff keep their counts or levels current.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

The master organisation can choose any studio. A studio organisation sees its own stock. Managers and admins can receive,
count and remove stock; the studio iPad can view it.

## On hand, reserved and available

For cakes and take-home bags, each row shows three numbers:

- **Here**: everything physically at the studio, including stock reserved for upcoming parties.
- **Reserved**: held for parties that have ordered it. Reserved stock stays in the freezer until the party.
- **Available to order**: here minus reserved. Only this can be ordered on the party form.

The day after a party, its reserved stock comes off automatically. When a booking is deleted, its reservation is given
back automatically.

<Aside type="tip" title="Counting the freezer is safe">
  Reserved cakes aren't labelled, and you don't need to know which is which. When you count, count everything in the
  freezer. The count should match **Here**.
</Aside>

## Receive the monthly delivery

<Steps>
  <ol>
    <li>A supervisor, area manager or franchisee checks the delivery against the docket.</li>
    <li>
      Select <strong>Receive cake & bag delivery</strong> and enter how many of each item arrived. Leave anything that
      didn't come at zero.
    </li>
    <li>
      Tick that you physically checked the delivery, then select <strong>Receive delivery</strong>.
    </li>
  </ol>
</Steps>

<Aside type="caution" title="Receive what arrived, never what's in the freezer">
  Enter the delivery from the docket, not by counting the freezer afterwards. Some of what is already there is reserved
  for upcoming parties.
</Aside>

## Count and remove

- **Count cakes & bags**: a stocktake of every cake and bag at once. Count everything, including reserved stock, and leave blank anything you didn't count. **Count** on a single row does the same for one item.
- **Count**: enter what is physically there. For cakes and bags, a count that doesn't match needs a reason, and the
  studio's owners are emailed. If the count leaves fewer than are reserved, some parties will be short, and the owners
  are told so they can sort it out.
- **Remove (thrown out)**: in the **…** menu. Use it when something spoils or is damaged, with a reason.
- **Stock history**: in the **…** menu. Every receive, count, removal, reservation and party, with who did it and why.

For party food, use **Receive** when stock arrives and **Count** after a stocktake. Level-based items use **Update
level**. **Mark count unknown** is for when a supply's count can no longer be trusted, and **Mark unused here** is for
when a studio doesn't use an item. Don't mark an item unused when it is only out of stock.

## Running low

Each item can have a running-low number. When stock drops to it, the row shows **Running low** and the studio's owners
are emailed once so they can reorder. For cakes and bags, running low is measured against what's still available to
order.

| Studio   | Owners emailed                             |
| -------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| Werribee | Paris                                      |
| Geelong  | The studio, area manager and Customer team |

## Setting up items

Only super admins create and edit items. Every cake and take-home bag must be linked to its Square cake design or bag
in **Ordered by customers as**: that's how the party form shows what's available and reserves it. Linked items start at zero at Werribee and Geelong, ready for the first
delivery, and are marked unused at other studios.
