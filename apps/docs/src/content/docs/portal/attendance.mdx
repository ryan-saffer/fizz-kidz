Attendance starts with a program and session. Holiday Program, After School Program, Preschool Program, and Play Lab
each have their own attendance view.

## Before the session

<Steps>
  1. Select the correct program from the Portal dashboard. 2. Choose the class or session. Confirm the date, time,
  studio, and class name. 3. Review every visible safety tag and expand the child row for contact and additional
  information.
</Steps>

Depending on the program, expanded details can include age, allergies, anaphylaxis and asthma plans, additional notes, parent
contact details, emergency contacts, booking type, photography permission, or pickup details.

<Aside type="danger" title="Safety information comes first">
  Read the child's allergy and medical plan information before signing them in. Holiday Program requires staff to view
  and verify each required anaphylaxis or asthma action plan before completing sign-in.
</Aside>

## What attendance actions do

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Preschool and Play Lab" icon="approve-check-circle">
    Use **Sign in** and **Sign out** to record attendance. Both screens provide undo actions. Preschool also shows
    signed-in and signed-out totals and supports switching directly between visible sessions.
  </Card>
  <Card title="After School Program" icon="approve-check-circle">
    Sign children in when they arrive. Sign-out records pickup and signature details for normal after-school sessions;
    lunchtime classes do not require a pickup signature.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Holiday Program" icon="approve-check-circle">
    Sign children in when they arrive. Use the undo action to correct an accidental sign-in.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

These are manual actions. Opening a child row does not mark attendance, and the system does not infer arrival or pickup.

## Correct attendance

- Confirm the exact studio, class, date, and time.
- Use the visible undo action for an accidental sign-in or sign-out.
- For Preschool, **Not attending** can be changed back with **Mark attending**.

Access to program attendance follows each person's Portal responsibilities. If the program is absent from your
dashboard, ask the person who manages Portal access.
