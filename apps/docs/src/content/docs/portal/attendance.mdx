Attendance starts with a program and session. Holiday Program, After School Program, and Preschool Program each have
their own attendance view.

## Before the session

<Steps>
  <ol>
    <li>Select the correct program from the Portal dashboard.</li>
    <li>Choose the class or session. Confirm the date, time, studio, and class name.</li>
    <li>Review every visible safety tag and expand the child row for contact and additional information.</li>
  </ol>
</Steps>

Depending on the program, expanded details can include age, allergies, anaphylaxis and asthma plans, additional notes, parent
contact details, emergency contacts, booking type, photography permission, or pickup details.

<Aside type="danger" title="Safety information comes first">
  Read the child's allergy and medical plan information before signing them in. Holiday Program requires staff to view
  and verify each required anaphylaxis or asthma action plan before completing sign-in.
</Aside>

## What attendance actions do

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Preschool Program" icon="approve-check-circle">
    Use **Sign in** and **Sign out** to record attendance, with undo actions for both. The screen also shows signed-in
    and signed-out totals and supports switching directly between visible sessions.
  </Card>
  <Card title="After School Program" icon="approve-check-circle">
    Sign children in when they arrive. Sign-out records who collected the child and their signature. Classes starting
    before 2 pm are lunchtime classes and are signed out without a pickup signature.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Holiday Program" icon="approve-check-circle">
    Sign children in when they arrive. Use the undo action to correct an accidental sign-in.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

These are manual actions. Opening a child row does not mark attendance, and the system does not infer arrival or pickup.

## Correct attendance

- Confirm the exact studio, class, date, and time.
- Use the visible undo action for an accidental sign-in or sign-out.
- For Preschool, **Not attending** can be changed back with **Mark attending**.
- For After School Program, open the child's row and use **More** for **Undo Sign In**, **Undo Sign Out**, **Mark Not
  Attending**, **Mark As Attending**, or **Not Continuing With Term**. See
  [Parent portal and attendance](/services/after-school-programs/parent-portal-and-attendance/).

Every program card shows on the dashboard, but opening one needs the matching Portal access. If you see **You are not
authorised to view this page**, ask the person who manages Portal access.
