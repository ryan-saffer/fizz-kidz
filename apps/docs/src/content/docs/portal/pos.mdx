**Sell products** is the studio's till for our kits, such as slime and bath bomb kits. Staff choose what the customer is
buying on the studio iPad, and the customer pays on the studio's Square Terminal.

Open **Sell products** from the Portal menu. It's available to studio iPads and super-admins. A studio iPad sells at its
own studio; super-admins choose the studio first.

## Make a sale

<Steps>
  <ol>
    <li>
      <h3>Select New sale</h3>
      <p>The sale opens full screen, with an order summary for the customer to follow.</p>
    </li>
    <li>
      <h3>Choose the products</h3>
      <p>
        Tap a product to add it, then use <strong>+</strong> and <strong>−</strong> to set how many. The products and
        prices come from Square.
      </p>
    </li>
    <li>
      <h3>Review and charge</h3>
      <p>
        The order summary shows Square's price, which is what's charged. Add a gift card or a discount if needed, then
        select <strong>Charge</strong>.
      </p>
    </li>
    <li>
      <h3>The customer pays on the terminal</h3>
      <p>
        They tap, insert or swipe their card. The terminal then offers them a receipt by email or text, which Square
        sends.
      </p>
    </li>
    <li>
      <h3>Start the next sale</h3>
      <p>
        Once it shows <strong>Payment received</strong>, select <strong>New sale</strong> for the next customer, or
        <strong>Done</strong>.
      </p>
    </li>
  </ol>
</Steps>

## Gift cards and discounts

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Gift card" icon="approve-check-circle">
    <p>
      Enter the card number with <strong>Use a gift card</strong>. Its balance comes off first and the terminal charges
      the rest.
    </p>
  </Card>
  <Card title="Discount" icon="pencil">
    <p>
      Enter a dollar amount and a reason with <strong>Add a discount</strong>, for example for a damaged box. The reason
      is kept for us and isn't shown to the customer.
    </p>
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## Good to know

- The sale is recorded in Square, and the products' stock goes down there automatically.
- The customer doesn't need to give their name. Their receipt goes to the email or mobile they enter on the terminal.
- A cancelled or timed-out charge takes nothing. Select **Send to terminal again** to retry, or **Edit the charge** to
  change the products first.
- Reloading the iPad during a charge doesn't lose it. Open **Sell products** again and it picks up where it was.

<Aside type="note" title="Refunds">
  Refund a sale with the approved Square refund process. The Portal doesn't refund sales.
</Aside>
