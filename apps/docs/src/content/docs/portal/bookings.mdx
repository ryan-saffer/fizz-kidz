Open **Parties, Events & Incursions** from the Portal dashboard. The page groups work by date, with separate views for
**Parties & Events** and **Incursions**.

## Find a booking

<Steps>
  <ol>
    <li>Choose the date using the booking page controls.</li>
    <li>If a studio filter is available, choose the relevant studio or all studios.</li>
    <li>
      Open the <strong>Parties &amp; Events</strong> or <strong>Incursions</strong> tab.
    </li>
    <li>Expand the booking to see its details and available actions.</li>
  </ol>
</Steps>

Party rows show the time, customer and birthday child, studio or mobile status, and useful flags such as self-catered or
**Paid**. Expanding a party shows the birthday child, parent, number of children, creations, food and notes, with its
actions underneath. A linked invitation also provides access to RSVPs. Events include a staff feedback form link.

## Create or update

Use **New Booking**, then choose **Party** or **Event**. On an existing booking, select **Edit**, make the change, and save
it.

Check names, email, mobile, studio, address, date, time, length, party type, food choice, and whether a confirmation should
be sent. When an existing party's time or length changes, the Portal emails the parent the revised time.

<Aside type="note" title="Confirmation email is controllable">
  New party bookings only send the booking confirmation when **Send the parent a confirmation email** is selected. An
  existing party also has a **Resend confirmation email** action.
</Aside>

## Collect payment

Once a studio party is over, **Collect payment** on the booking charges it on the studio's Square Terminal. See
[Collect party payment](/services/birthday-parties/collect-payment/).

## Delete carefully

Deleting a party removes the booking and its linked invitation. The delete flow asks for a lost reason.

<Aside type="caution" title="Deletion is not customer communication">
  The party deletion workflow does not send a cancellation email. Confirm the customer communication separately before
  deleting the record.
</Aside>

## Automatic party follow-up

The Portal sends party forms, reminders, cake forms, guest-count messages, and feedback emails at the appropriate times.
Keep booking dates and customer email addresses current.
