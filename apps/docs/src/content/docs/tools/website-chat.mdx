Frankie is the AI chat assistant on www.fizzkidz.com.au. Frankie answers customers' questions, links them to the right page, and takes enquiries for the team.

## What Frankie does

- Answers questions using what's on the website, plus extra information from the team.
- Points customers to book online for Holiday Programs, the Preschool Program and After School Programs.
- Takes the enquiry for birthday parties, incursions, activations and events, and for any question Frankie can't answer.
- Can't check availability or confirm bookings. Frankie tells customers the team will get back to them with options.

## How enquiries arrive

An enquiry from Frankie arrives like one from the website contact form: the same Zoho records, the same email to bookings@fizzkidz.com.au, and a confirmation email to the customer.

To spot one:

- The enquiry ends with **(Left by Frankie, the website chat assistant)**.
- The whole chat is attached in Zoho as a **Website chat transcript** note, on the deal, or on the contact when the enquiry has no deal.
- Frankie only insists on a name, email and mobile, plus a rough date for bookings. Anything else the customer didn't give is listed as **Not provided yet**.

Read the transcript before following up. It shows exactly what the customer asked and what Frankie told them.

## Unconfirmed enquiries

Before sending, Frankie shows the customer their details with **Send enquiry**, **Change something** and **Don't send** buttons.

A customer who gives their name, email and mobile but leaves without choosing is still worth a follow-up. Their enquiry is sent for them about 30 to 45 minutes after they leave, and says **The customer left the chat before confirming these details, so please double-check them.** The customer still gets the confirmation email.

A customer who taps Don't send is never passed on.

## Read chat transcripts

Super-admins can read every chat in the Portal under **Ops & admin → Chat Transcripts**, including chats that didn't lead to an enquiry. Transcripts include customers' contact details, so only super-admins can see them. Super-admins can also delete transcripts from the table.

## Change what Frankie knows

- **Website content:** Frankie keeps a copy of what's on the website. It's updated whenever the website changes.
- **Team information:** answers that aren't on the website, like policies and common questions. To add or change something, send it to the tech team.

If Frankie gets something wrong, note the time of the chat and let the tech team know. Changes reach Frankie with the next update.
