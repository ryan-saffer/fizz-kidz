Acuity is the scheduling system behind program bookings. It holds session times, studio calendars, appointment records, and available capacity for holiday programs, Play Lab, after school programs, preschool programs, and other configured classes.

<Aside type="caution" title="Acuity does not own payments">
  Payments, invoices, gift cards, receipts, and refunds are handled in [Square](/tools/square). Never use Acuity's
  checkout tools for a Fizz Kidz booking.
</Aside>

## How a booking works

1. The Portal reads current sessions and availability from Acuity.
2. The customer selects a session in the Fizz Kidz booking flow.
3. Where payment is required at checkout, Square processes it.
4. The Portal creates the appointment in Acuity and includes the booking details staff need.
5. The customer receives the relevant confirmation and self-service links.

Customers normally use the Fizz Kidz booking flow, not Acuity's public scheduler. Some confirmation emails link to an Acuity-hosted page for supported changes or cancellations.

## What updates automatically

- Portal bookings create Acuity appointments and reduce available capacity.
- Capacity is confirmed when a customer completes a capacity-sensitive booking.
- Holiday Program, Play Lab, and current Preschool Program cancellations refund through [Square](/tools/square).
- Holiday Program check-ins, reschedules, and cancellations update the related [Zoho](/tools/zoho) record.

<Aside type="note" title="Refunds depend on the program">
  Only the supported programs listed above refund automatically. Other refunds are completed in Square.
</Aside>

## Use Acuity

Sign in at [secure.acuityscheduling.com/appointments.php](https://secure.acuityscheduling.com/appointments.php).

- Open **Calendar**, then use **Week View** or **Month View** to see sessions and capacity.
- Open an appointment to see its session time, customer details, appointment type, and studio calendar.
- Use Square for payments and refunds.

Changes to Acuity classes can immediately affect what customers can see or book in the Portal. Make schedule or capacity changes carefully.

For Acuity's own interface, see the [Acuity Help Center](https://help.acuityscheduling.com/hc/en-us).
