Fizz Kidz uses separate calendars for confirmed work and tentative party holds. Add the calendars you need while signed in to your Fizz Kidz Google account.

## Calendar links

### Automatic calendars

Confirmed party bookings, events, and incursions are created, updated, and removed here by the Portal. Incursions appear on the Events calendar.

Make booking changes in the Portal. The automatic calendar, customer communications, and [Zoho](/tools/zoho) follow the Portal record.

### Tentative party calendars

These calendars are manual placeholders for possible party times. A tentative hold is not a confirmed booking and is not linked to the automatic calendar.

- Add, update, and remove tentative holds manually.
- Check both calendars when reviewing whether a time is free.
- Remove a tentative hold when it is no longer needed. Confirmed bookings do not remove manual holds.
