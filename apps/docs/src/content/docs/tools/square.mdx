Square is the payment system behind Fizz Kidz checkout. It records customers, orders, card and gift-card payments, invoices, receipts, and refunds.

<Aside type="note" title="Square records money, not availability">
  Program schedules, appointments, and capacity live in [Acuity](/tools/acuity). Confirmed parties and events live in
  the Portal and appear on [automatic calendars](/tools/calendars#automatic-calendars).
</Aside>

## Where Square is used

- Holiday Program and the current Preschool Program checkout create and pay Square orders through the Portal.
- Gift cards can be applied to supported online orders before any remaining card balance is charged.
- After School Program invoicing creates Square invoices that are emailed to the customer.
- Party extras can send the customer to a Square payment link.
- Studio parties are charged on the studio's Square Terminal from the Portal with
  [Collect party payment](/services/birthday-parties/collect-payment/).
- Our kits are sold on the same terminal with [Sell products](/portal/pos/). Sales update the products' stock in
  Square.
- Supported cancellations create Square refunds through the Fizz Kidz booking flow.

Customers may see a Square receipt, invoice, or hosted payment page even though they started in a Fizz Kidz form.

## Automatic and manual work

**Automatic:** the Portal creates customers and orders, applies supported discounts or gift cards, processes payment, sends charges to the studio's terminal, publishes supported invoices, and records supported refunds.

**Manual:** staff use Square for other refunds, payment links, invoice management, and payment enquiries.

Open an order to see its line items, discounts, gift-card and card payments, receipts, and refunds. Open an invoice to see its delivery and payment status.

## Studio terminal

Each studio's Square Terminal is paired with the Portal, which sends it party and product charges. While paired, it doesn't run the Square Point of Sale app. See [Studio Square Terminal](/portal/studio-terminal/) to pair or replace it.
