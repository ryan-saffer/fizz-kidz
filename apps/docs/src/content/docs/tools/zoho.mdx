---

Zoho is the customer relationship management system. It brings together enquiries, contacts, children, deals, program interest, and team follow-up.

## What Zoho records

- Website enquiry forms create or update contacts and create deals or leads where relevant.
- Confirmed party and event bookings confirm or update the related deal.
- A paid party records its amount on the party's deal, with a **Party payment** note of what was charged.
- Holiday Program, Play Lab, After School, and Preschool Program bookings add customer and child details.
- Supported party, event, and Holiday Program changes update related records.

The details recorded depend on the service and customer journey.

## Use Zoho for follow-up

- Record calls, emails, sales notes, and next actions.
- Use deals and lead stages to manage customer follow-up.
- Keep customer and child details current when the customer provides new information.
- Search by customer email to find records created by connected forms and bookings.

## Tool boundaries

Zoho manages the customer relationship and follow-up. [Acuity](/tools/acuity) manages program appointments and capacity, [Square](/tools/square) manages money, and the Portal manages confirmed parties and events.
