## Find the right system

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Fizz Kidz Portal" icon="setting">
    The staff dashboard for bookings, attendance, payments at the studio, reports, discount codes, inventory, and other
    Fizz workflows. What appears depends on your studio and role. [Learn the basics](/portal/basics/).
  </Card>
  <Card title="Acuity Scheduling" icon="clock">
    Holds class schedules, appointments, and capacity for programs. The Portal reads and updates this information for
    many day-to-day tasks. [How Acuity fits in](/tools/acuity/).
  </Card>
  <Card title="Square" icon="document">
    Handles customer payments and gift cards, including the studio's Square Terminal. The Portal coordinates booking
    details around those payments. [How Square fits in](/tools/square/).
  </Card>
  <Card title="Fizz technology" icon="puzzle">
    Start with a simple overview of the Portal and the other tools you may see. [See Fizz technology at a
    glance](/start-here/how-fizz-tech-works/).
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
