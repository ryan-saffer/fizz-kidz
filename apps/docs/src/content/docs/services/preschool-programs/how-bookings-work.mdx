Preschool Program checkout lets families book and pay for individual dates or a whole term.{' '}

<strong>Square records payment</strong>; <strong>Acuity records each child's session</strong>.

## Customer journey

<Steps>
  <ol>
    <li>
      <h3>Choose a studio and sessions</h3>
      <p>
        Dates come from Acuity and are grouped by weekday, start time and term. Families can select individual dates or
        use <strong>Book into entire term</strong> when it is available.
      </p>
    </li>
    <li>
      <h3>Add family details</h3>
      <p>
        The form collects parent, child, emergency contact, allergy, anaphylaxis-plan and additional information. Totals
        update when the number of children changes.
      </p>
    </li>
    <li>
      <h3>Review pricing and pay</h3>
      <p>
        The 20% whole-term discount is applied first, then one discount code, then a Fizz Kidz gift card. Any balance is
        paid by card, Apple Pay or Google Pay.
      </p>
    </li>
    <li>
      <h3>Receive confirmation</h3>
      <p>
        The success screen shows <strong>Booking confirmed</strong> and says the Square receipt will arrive shortly. The
        Fizz Kidz confirmation email lists every child/session, the location, any whole-term discount, a receipt link
        when available, and individual <strong>Change / Cancel</strong> links.
      </p>
    </li>
  </ol>
</Steps>

## Booking outcome

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Current availability" icon="warning">
    Availability and prices are confirmed at checkout. If a selected session no longer has enough places for every
    child, the family can choose another session.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Payment summary" icon="seti:lock">
    The order includes every child and session. Whole-term and code discounts reduce the total, and gift card and card
    payments can share the balance.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Session bookings" icon="information">
    Each child has a separate appointment for each session, carrying the contact and safety details used by attendance.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Confirmation" icon="email">
    Each management link controls one child/session, even when the family books a whole term in one checkout.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## Discounts and gift cards

- A percentage code is calculated after the 20% whole-term reduction.
- A fixed code cannot exceed the already-discounted subtotal.
- One discount code can be applied. It may also be limited by expiry, available uses or one use per customer.
- A gift card pays the discounted total first; another payment method covers any remainder.
- See [Whole-term bookings](/services/preschool-programs/whole-term-bookings/) for eligibility and cancellation
  repricing.
