Preschool attendance is read from Acuity. One paid checkout can create many rows because every child/session is a
separate appointment.

## Attendance labels

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Not signed in" icon="information">
    <Badge text="No attendance label" variant="default" size="small" />
    <br />
    The child is booked but has not yet been signed in.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Signed in" icon="approve-check-circle">
    <Badge text="Checked in" variant="tip" size="small" />
    <br />
    The row turns green and is included in the Signed in count.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Signed out" icon="rocket">
    <Badge text="Checked out" variant="note" size="small" />
    <br />
    The row becomes muted and is included in the Signed out count.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Not attending" icon="warning">
    <Badge text="Not attending" variant="caution" size="small" />
    <br />
    The Portal shows the saved Acuity label. Staff can sign the child in if they arrive or use{' '}
    <strong>Mark attending</strong> to clear it.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## Staff flow

<Steps>
  <ol>
    <li>
      <h3>Choose studio and session</h3>
      <p>
        The selector shows today's and upcoming sessions. Turn on <strong>Show previous sessions</strong> to find an
        earlier date.
      </p>
    </li>
    <li>
      <h3>Review the row</h3>
      <p>Expand it for parent and emergency contacts, allergies, additional information and any anaphylaxis plan.</p>
    </li>
    <li>
      <h3>Sign in, then sign out</h3>
      <p>Each action replaces the attendance label in Acuity and updates the row and summary count.</p>
    </li>
    <li>
      <h3>Correct a mistake</h3>
      <p>
        <strong>Undo sign in</strong> returns to no attendance label. <strong>Undo sign out</strong> returns to Checked
        in.
      </p>
    </li>
  </ol>
</Steps>

<Aside type="caution" title="Safety details come from the appointment">
  <Badge text="Anaphylaxis" variant="caution" size="small" /> <Badge text="Allergies" variant="caution" size="small" />
  <br />
  These details are drawn from the booking. Open the row and review the plan before sign-in. The tags highlight the
  submitted details but do not replace reviewing the plan.
</Aside>
