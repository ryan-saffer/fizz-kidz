The booking confirmation has a <strong>Change / Cancel</strong> link beside every child/session. It opens that
appointment in the Portal, with the session details and options to cancel or reschedule. No login is needed. Acuity
manages the appointment; Square continues to hold the original order and payment.

## Change or cancel?

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Reschedule at least 48 hours before" icon="information">
    Moves one child's appointment to another session in the same term at the same studio, on any day. There is no new
    charge or refund, and a whole-term booking keeps its 20% discount. The family gets a short email showing the
    previous and new session.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Cancel at least 48 hours before" icon="approve-check-circle">
    <Badge text="Automatic" variant="tip" size="small" />
    <br />
    The remaining booking is repriced and any eligible difference is refunded.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Cancel inside 48 hours" icon="warning">
    <Badge text="No automatic refund" variant="caution" size="small" />
    <br />
    Rescheduling is disabled. The family can still cancel before the session starts and is emailed, but no refund is
    processed.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

<Aside type="tip" title="The refund is shown before cancelling">
  The cancel confirmation shows the exact refund. When cancelling removes the whole-term discount, it explains why the
  refund is small or $0.
</Aside>

Links in confirmations sent before the Portal page launched still open Acuity.

## How the refund is calculated

For cancellations at least 48 hours before the session, the refund is the amount paid minus the value of all sessions
still booked. Whole-term eligibility and the original discount code are included in that calculation.

- Cancelling a date from a whole-term booking reprices the remaining term dates at the standard session price, so the
  refund can be less than one session's price, or $0. See
  [Whole-term bookings](/services/preschool-programs/whole-term-bookings/).
- Split payments are refunded to the original gift card and card, up to the amount paid with each.
- The cancellation email states the refunded amount when a refund applies.
