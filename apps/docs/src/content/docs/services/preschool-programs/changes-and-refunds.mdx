The booking confirmation gives the family a separate <strong>Change / Cancel</strong> link for every child/session.
Acuity manages the appointment; Square continues to hold the original order and payment.

## Change or cancel?

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Change a session" icon="information">
    Rescheduling changes the Acuity appointment. It does not automatically create a new Square charge or refund.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Cancel at least 48 hours before" icon="approve-check-circle">
    <Badge text="Automatic" variant="tip" size="small" />
    <br />
    The remaining booking is repriced and any eligible difference is refunded.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Cancel inside 48 hours" icon="warning">
    <Badge text="No automatic refund" variant="caution" size="small" />
    <br />
    The appointment is cancelled and the family is emailed, but no refund is processed.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## How the refund is calculated

For cancellations at least 48 hours before the session, the refund is the amount paid minus the value of all sessions
still booked. Whole-term eligibility and the original discount code are included in that calculation.

- Cancelling a date from a whole-term booking reprices the remaining term dates at the standard session price, so the
  refund can be less than one session's price, or $0. See
  [Whole-term bookings](/services/preschool-programs/whole-term-bookings/).
- Split payments are refunded to the original gift card and card, up to the amount paid with each.
- The cancellation email states the refunded amount when a refund applies.
