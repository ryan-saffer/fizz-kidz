Staff start the continuation step from **After School Program Invoicing**. Select the enrolments, then choose
**Action** > **Send Enrolment Email** to ask each family whether their child will continue for the term.

<Steps>
  <ol>
    <li>
      <h3>
        Staff send the continuation email <Badge text="Manual start" variant="note" size="small" />
      </h3>
      <p>
        The email includes the child, program, term total, and separate links for continuing or not continuing. The{' '}
        <strong>Enrolment Email Sent</strong> column changes to <strong>YES</strong>.
      </p>
    </li>
    <li>
      <h3>The family chooses yes or no</h3>
      <p>
        Opening either link records the family's choice. The <strong>Continuing?</strong> column then shows{' '}
        <strong>YES</strong>, <strong>NO</strong>, or <strong>UNKNOWN</strong>.
      </p>
    </li>
    <li>
      <h3>Staff complete the matching action</h3>
      <p>
        For a yes, use <strong>Action</strong> &gt; <strong>Send Invoice</strong>. For a no, the bookings team is
        alerted, and staff still use <strong>Action</strong> &gt; <strong>Unenroll From Term</strong>.
      </p>
    </li>
  </ol>
</Steps>

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="YES: continuing" icon="approve-check-circle">
    The family sees that their term place is confirmed and that an invoice will follow. Their choice alone does not send
    the invoice; staff send it with **Send Invoice**.
  </Card>
  <Card title="NO: not continuing" icon="information">
    The Portal records the choice and alerts the bookings team. Check-in shows the child as not continuing, but they
    stay booked into the term until staff use **Unenroll From Term**.
  </Card>
  <Card title="UNKNOWN: no choice yet" icon="warning">
    The family has not selected yes or no. Staff decide whether to send another continuation email or contact them
    directly.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

Sending an invoice also sets **Continuing?** and **Enrolment Email Sent** to **YES**, even if the family never answered
the email. Staff can also record **Not Continuing With Term** from the check-in **More** menu, which alerts the bookings
team in the same way.

<Aside type="caution" title="A no response is not the final cancellation">
  Recording <strong>not continuing</strong> does not cancel the child's classes, void an unpaid invoice, or remove the
  enrolment from the active lists. Use <strong>Unenroll From Term</strong> to complete those changes.
</Aside>

## Completing an unenrolment

**Unenroll From Term**:

- Cancels the child's remaining after-school classes.
- Cancels the unpaid invoice when it is still cancellable.
- Removes the enrolment from the active check-in and invoicing lists.
- Optionally sends the family an unenrolment confirmation email.

Paid invoices are not refunded by this action. If a refund is appropriate, make it separately in Square. A partial
refund still shows as **PAID**, while a full refund shows as **UNPAID** and the parent portal offers **Pay Now**. Don't
send a new invoice in that case; record the child and program and report it. See
[Invoicing](/services/after-school-programs/invoicing/) for invoice statuses.

For the earlier steps, see [Enrolments and waitlists](/services/after-school-programs/enrolments-and-waitlists/).
