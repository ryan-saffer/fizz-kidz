---
title: How it works
description: The after-school program journey from enrolment and free trial to invoicing and attendance.
---

import { Aside, Card, CardGrid, Steps } from '@astrojs/starlight/components'

Families enrol online without paying, try the program for free, and then decide whether to continue for the term. Staff
send the invoice to families who continue and sign children in and out each week.

## Journey at a glance

<Steps>
  <ol>
    <li>
      <h3>The family chooses a program</h3>
      <p>
        The enrolment form shows the school and studio programs currently open for booking, with the term length, total
        price, and whether the class has space.
      </p>
    </li>
    <li>
      <h3>The form offers enrolment or the waitlist</h3>
      <p>
        If the class has space, the family completes the full enrolment form. If it is full, they see a shorter waitlist
        form instead. See{' '}
        <a href="/services/after-school-programs/enrolments-and-waitlists/">Enrolments and waitlists</a>.
      </p>
    </li>
    <li>
      <h3>The child is enrolled without a card</h3>
      <p>
        No card details are collected. The child is booked into the remaining term sessions, and the family receives an
        enrolment confirmation and parent portal link.
      </p>
    </li>
    <li>
      <h3>The family tries the program</h3>
      <p>The free trial is one of the term sessions already booked. The family is not invoiced when they enrol.</p>
    </li>
    <li>
      <h3>Staff ask whether they are continuing</h3>
      <p>
        Staff send a continuation email and the family's answer appears in the Portal. A yes is ready for invoicing; a
        no alerts the bookings team for follow-up. See{' '}
        <a href="/services/after-school-programs/after-the-trial/">After the trial</a>.
      </p>
    </li>
    <li>
      <h3>Square handles payment</h3>
      <p>
        Staff select the number of weeks and send the invoice. Square emails the family, and the Portal shows whether it
        has been paid. See <a href="/services/after-school-programs/invoicing/">Invoicing</a>.
      </p>
    </li>
    <li>
      <h3>Attendance continues week by week</h3>
      <p>
        Parents can mark an absence and update pickup people in their portal. Staff sign children in and out at each
        class. See{' '}
        <a href="/services/after-school-programs/parent-portal-and-attendance/">Parent portal and attendance</a>.
      </p>
    </li>
  </ol>
</Steps>

## Tools you will see

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Portal" icon="open-book">
    Where staff manage enrolments, continuation choices, invoices, and attendance. Each child has one enrolment.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Acuity" icon="clock">
    Holds the class dates and places. Enrolling books the child into every remaining class in the term.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Square" icon="approve-check-circle">
    Sends the term invoice after the free trial and takes the payment.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

<Aside type="note" title="One child, one enrolment">
  When a family enrols more than one child, each child gets their own enrolment, class bookings, parent portal link,
  continuation choice, and invoice.
</Aside>
