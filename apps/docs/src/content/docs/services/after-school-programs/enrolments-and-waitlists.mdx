import { Aside, Card, CardGrid, Steps } from '@astrojs/starlight/components'

## When a class has space

<Steps>
  <ol>
    <li>
      <h3>The family chooses an open program</h3>
      <p>
        The form only offers after-school programs that are currently open for booking, with each program's price,
        upcoming classes, and whether it has space.
      </p>
    </li>
    <li>
      <h3>The family enters enrolment details</h3>
      <p>
        The form collects the parent, child, emergency contact, allergy and support information, photography permission,
        and approved pickup people. No credit card details are requested.
      </p>
    </li>
    <li>
      <h3>The child's term place is booked</h3>
      <p>
        The child is booked into every remaining published class. Their Portal enrolment holds the session list and
        per-week price.
      </p>
    </li>
    <li>
      <h3>The family receives two useful emails</h3>
      <p>
        The enrolment confirmation lists the sessions, location, term price, and number of weeks. A separate email gives
        the family their parent portal link.
      </p>
    </li>
  </ol>
</Steps>

<Aside type="tip" title="The first booking is a real term enrolment">
  The system creates all remaining term appointments so the child's place is held. The free trial changes when payment
  is requested, not which appointments are initially created.
</Aside>

## When a class is full

When the selected class has no space, the full enrolment form is replaced by a waitlist form.

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="What is saved" icon="add-document">
    The program, parent name, parent email, parent mobile, and child name are added to the after-school waitlist sheet.
  </Card>
  <Card title="What is not created" icon="warning">
    There is no Portal enrolment, Acuity appointment, parent portal, or Square invoice. The success message only
    confirms that the family joined the waitlist.
  </Card>
  <Card title="What happens next" icon="email">
    The waitlist does not automatically promote or enrol a child. The team contacts the family if a place becomes
    available and completes the enrolment separately.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
