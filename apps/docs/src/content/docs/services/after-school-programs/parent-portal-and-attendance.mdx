Each child has a parent portal. The link is emailed when the child enrols, and staff can also open it from the child's
expanded details in **After School Program Invoicing**.

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Enrolment and invoice" icon="open-book">
    The portal shows the program, child, expected term price, and invoice status. An unpaid invoice has a payment link;
    a paid invoice can still be viewed.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Weekly attendance" icon="clock">
    The parent can turn attendance off for an individual class. Staff see the child as not attending at check-in.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Pickup people" icon="heart">
    The parent can add or remove approved pickup people. Staff see the updated list at pickup.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

<Aside type="note" title="An absence is not a cancellation">
  Turning off one week only marks the child as not attending that class. It does not remove the booking, change the
  invoice, or create a credit or refund.
</Aside>

## Staff attendance flow

Open **Programs** > **After School Program** and select the class. Turn on **Show past classes** to find an earlier
date.

<Steps>
  <ol>
    <li>
      <h3>Review the child's current details</h3>
      <p>
        Staff can see absences, continuation choices, allergies, anaphylaxis information, photography permission,
        support notes, emergency contact details, and pickup people.
      </p>
    </li>
    <li>
      <h3>Sign the child in</h3>
      <p>
        Use <strong>Sign In</strong> when the child arrives. Children marked not attending do not show the sign-in
        button.
      </p>
    </li>
    <li>
      <h3>Record pickup</h3>
      <p>
        Use <strong>Sign Out</strong>, select the person collecting the child, and capture their signature. The pickup
        person, signature, and time are saved for that class.
      </p>
    </li>
  </ol>
</Steps>

The sign-out choices are the enrolled parent, the pickup people added in the parent portal, and **Fizz Kidz Staff**.
Signing out as Fizz Kidz Staff also needs the staff member's name and the reason. Classes starting before 2 pm are
lunchtime classes, so **Sign Out** records them without a pickup signature.

## Other staff actions

Open the child's row and use **More** to correct attendance or record a change.

| Action                       | What it does                                                            |
| ---------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Mark Not Attending**       | Marks the child absent for this class.                                  |
| **Mark As Attending**        | Clears a not-attending mark so the child can be signed in.              |
| **Not Continuing With Term** | Records that the family is not continuing and alerts the bookings team. |
| **Undo Sign In**             | Returns a signed-in child to not signed in.                             |
| **Undo Sign Out**            | Returns a signed-out child to signed in.                                |

For full-term removal rather than a single absence, follow [After the trial](/services/after-school-programs/after-the-trial/).
For payment questions, see [Invoicing](/services/after-school-programs/invoicing/).
