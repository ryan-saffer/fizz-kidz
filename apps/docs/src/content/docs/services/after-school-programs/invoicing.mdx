After-school invoicing begins after the free trial. Staff send invoices from **Ops & admin** > **After School Program
Invoicing**, which needs admin access. Square emails the invoice to the family and takes the payment.

## Sending an invoice

<Steps>
  <ol>
    <li>
      <h3>Choose the program and enrolments</h3>
      <p>
        The page lists the program's active enrolments with their continuation choice and current invoice status. Select
        the children to invoice.
      </p>
    </li>
    <li>
      <h3>Select the number of weeks</h3>
      <p>
        Choose <strong>Action</strong> &gt; <strong>Send Invoice</strong> and pick between 1 and 10 weeks. The Portal
        multiplies that by the per-week price saved when the child enrolled, so a mid-term enrolment can be billed for
        fewer weeks.
      </p>
    </li>
    <li>
      <h3>Square emails the invoice</h3>
      <p>
        The family receives an invoice with a card payment link. It is due three days after it is sent and includes
        three scheduled reminders.
      </p>
    </li>
    <li>
      <h3>The enrolment is marked as continuing</h3>
      <p>
        Sending an invoice sets <strong>Continuing?</strong> and <strong>Enrolment Email Sent</strong> to{' '}
        <strong>YES</strong>. Staff and the parent portal then show the invoice status.
      </p>
    </li>
  </ol>
</Steps>

<Aside type="caution" title="Choose the weeks before sending">
  The number of weeks is not taken from the child's booked classes, so check it before sending. Sending again creates a
  new invoice and cancels the previous one when it is still cancellable. The Portal blocks <strong>Send Invoice</strong>{' '}
  with an <strong>Invalid Selection</strong> message if any selected child has already paid.
</Aside>

<Aside type="note" title="Reminders are automatic">
  Square sends the three reminders using the schedule included with the invoice. Staff do not need to send them
  separately.
</Aside>

## Portal statuses

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="NOT SENT" icon="information">
    No invoice has been sent. The parent portal shows "Invoice not yet sent" and the expected full-term price, with no
    payment button.
  </Card>
  <Card title="UNPAID" icon="warning">
    The invoice has been sent but not paid. Staff can open the invoice in Square; the parent sees a{' '}
    <strong>Pay Now</strong> button.
  </Card>
  <Card title="PAID" icon="approve-check-circle">
    The invoice is paid. Partially refunded invoices also show as <strong>PAID</strong>.
  </Card>
  <Card title="VOID" icon="seti:lock">
    The invoice was cancelled. Staff can still open it in Square; the parent portal shows "Invoice not yet sent".
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## Unenrolments and refunds

<Badge text="Portal" variant="tip" size="small" /> <Badge text="Square" variant="caution" size="small" />

**Unenroll From Term** cancels the child's classes, removes the enrolment from the active lists, and cancels an unpaid
invoice when it is still cancellable.

It does **not** refund a paid invoice. Refunds are made separately in Square:

- A partially refunded invoice still shows as **PAID**.
- A fully refunded invoice shows as **UNPAID**, and the parent portal offers **Pay Now**.

If a fully refunded invoice shows as unpaid, don't send a new invoice. Record the child, program, and what the Portal
shows, and report it so it can be fixed.

<Aside type="note" title="Do not use an absence to adjust an invoice">
  A week marked as not attending remains part of the enrolment and does not create a credit or refund. Attendance and
  billing are separate. See{' '}
  <a href="/services/after-school-programs/parent-portal-and-attendance/">Parent portal and attendance</a>.
</Aside>

See [After the trial](/services/after-school-programs/after-the-trial/) for the continuation step that comes before
invoicing.
