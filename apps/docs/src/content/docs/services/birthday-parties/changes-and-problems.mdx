Use the booking menu for common customer requests and make booking changes in the Portal rather than editing the Google
Calendar event directly.

## Send another email or link

### Booking confirmation

1. Confirm that **Send the parent a confirmation email** was selected when booked.
2. Check the parent email saved in the Portal.
3. Correct the email address if needed.
4. Use **Resend confirmation email** and confirm the address shown in the prompt.

### Updated party details

Only a date, start-time or party-length change automatically sends an updated confirmation. Address, contact, food,
notes, creation and add-on edits do not. Contact the family directly for those changes.

### Party Form or reminder

Use **Copy form link > Party form** from the booking menu and send it directly. The reminder depends on the booking still being
marked as missing its Party Form; it is not a general resend button.

### RSVP details

Guests cannot edit a submitted RSVP themselves. The host or a signed-in team member can update the response from the
guest-list view. Contact the guest directly if they need another confirmation.

### Party payment receipt

The family is emailed an itemised receipt when the party is paid. If they need it again, open the booking and select its
**Paid** amount to open the Square receipt, then share that link.

## Details changed after a form submission

- A repeat Party Form emails the studio a before-and-after comparison.
- Repeated take-home bag and product quantities are added to existing quantities.
- Removing a previously selected unpaid addition may require a manual Portal correction.
- Changing an existing RSVP is silent and does not email the guest or reverse Zoho data.
- Changing the party date or length through the booking editor updates Calendar and sends the parent an email.

For paid-order or party payment corrections, use the approved Square refund or correction process and contact the supplier directly when
their order needs to change. Editing the Portal does not refund a payment or change an order already sent to a supplier.

## Cancel or delete a party

Deleting a booking removes the Calendar event, invitation, RSVPs and booking, and marks a linked Zoho deal as lost. It
does **not**:

- notify the customer;
- collect a cancellation fee;
- refund Square payments or paid add-ons; or
- cancel an order directly with the cake supplier.

Complete those customer, payment and supplier steps separately as your approved process requires.
