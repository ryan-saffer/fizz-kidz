This is the technology journey from booking to follow-up. It explains what customers receive and what the systems do in
the background. It is not a party-day run sheet.

## At a glance

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Booking created" icon="approve-check-circle">
    <p>
      <Badge text="Portal" variant="tip" size="small" /> <Badge text="Calendar" variant="note" size="small" />
      <br />
      Portal saves the booking and creates the studio or mobile-party Google Calendar event.
    </p>
  </Card>
  <Card title="Confirmation" icon="email">
    <p>
      <Badge text="Optional at booking" variant="caution" size="small" />
      <br />
      If <strong>Send the parent a confirmation email</strong> was selected, the family receives the date, time,
      location, contact details and useful links.
    </p>
  </Card>
  <Card title="Cake and goodies" icon="add-document">
    <p>
      Eligible studio parties may receive an earlier order reminder. Cakes are not offered for mobile parties or
      Geelong; customers who already ordered a cake, take-home bags or products are skipped by this reminder.
    </p>
  </Card>
  <Card title="Party Form" icon="open-book">
    <p>
      A scheduled email asks for final numbers, creations, food choices and notes. A later reminder is sent only while
      the booking is still marked as missing its Party Form.
    </p>
  </Card>
  <Card title="Form processing" icon="rocket">
    <p>
      The Portal booking is updated, the customer receives a summary, and relevant studio or supplier emails are sent.
      Paid items go through Square first.
    </p>
  </Card>
  <Card title="Party payment" icon="approve-check-circle">
    <p>
      Staff charge the party on the studio's Square Terminal once it's over, and the family is emailed an itemised
      receipt.
    </p>
  </Card>
  <Card title="Review request" icon="star">
    <p>
      The automatic follow-up selects parties from the previous day and sends the family the review link for their
      studio.
    </p>
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## Journey in order

<Steps>
  <ol>
    <li>
      <h3>Team member creates the booking</h3>
      <p>
        The Portal saves the party, creates its Google Calendar event, and creates or updates the parent, child and
        confirmed deal in Zoho CRM.
      </p>
    </li>
    <li>
      <h3>Family receives the booking confirmation</h3>
      <p>
        This email is only automatic when <strong>Send the parent a confirmation email</strong> was selected.
      </p>
      <p>
        It includes the party date and time, address, food-package choice for studio parties, the relevant Fizz contact,
        cancellation terms, invitation link and studio photo where applicable. Eligible bookings also receive the
        cake/take-home order link.
      </p>
      <p>
        Team members can use <strong>Resend confirmation email</strong> from the booking menu when a family needs
        another copy.
      </p>
    </li>
    <li>
      <h3>Eligible families receive the early order reminder</h3>
      <p>
        The cake-form job targets eligible parties roughly three to four weeks away. It skips a booking if a cake,
        take-home bag or other paid product is already recorded.
      </p>
      <p>
        The link opens the same hosted Paperform used for party details, but in cake/order mode. The form is prefilled
        from the current Portal booking when it opens.
      </p>
    </li>
    <li>
      <h3>Family receives the Party Form</h3>
      <p>
        The scheduled Tuesday workflow selects parties in the following Tuesday-to-Tuesday window. For normal weekend
        parties this is about one to two weeks before the event. The email asks the family to complete the form by the
        next Tuesday.
      </p>
      <p>
        A separate Monday workflow reminds bookings in the upcoming Tuesday-to-Tuesday window only when
        <code>Party Form filled in</code> is still false.
      </p>
      <p>
        The send time is scheduled automatically, so describe the timing to families as a date window rather than a
        precise hour.
      </p>
    </li>
    <li>
      <h3>Square handles paid add-ons</h3>
      <p>
        If the form includes a Fizz cake, take-home bags or products, the customer is sent to a Square checkout before
        the form is marked complete. If none of those items is selected, it goes straight to completion.
      </p>
      <p>
        Food additions and the party itself aren't charged by this checkout. They're paid at the party, and the Portal
        labels the additions <strong>Pay at party</strong>.
      </p>
    </li>
    <li>
      <h3>The booking and inboxes are updated</h3>
      <p>
        Form completion updates final numbers, creations, food choice, questions and purchased items in the Portal. It
        can also trigger studio emails for a repeated form, food-package change, too many creations, a parent question,
        party packs or take-home items.
      </p>
      <p>
        Cake orders are emailed to <code>orders@birthdaycakeshop.com.au</code> with the studio copied. New take-home bag
        orders are also emailed to that supplier. The customer receives the appropriate Party Form or order summary.
      </p>
    </li>
    <li>
      <h3>Staff collect the party payment</h3>
      <p>
        Once the party is over, staff use <strong>Collect payment</strong> on the booking to charge the party and its
        food additions on the studio's Square Terminal. The family receives an itemised receipt by email, and the
        booking shows <strong>Paid</strong>. See{' '}
        <a href="/services/birthday-parties/collect-payment/">Collect party payment</a>.
      </p>
    </li>
    <li>
      <h3>Family receives the review request</h3>
      <p>
        The follow-up workflow looks for parties held the previous day and sends the parent the location-specific Google
        review link. Replies go back to Fizz Kidz for private follow-up.
      </p>
    </li>
  </ol>
</Steps>
