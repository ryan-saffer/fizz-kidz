Once a studio party is over, staff charge it from the booking on the studio iPad. The Portal works out the price from
Square, sends the charge to the studio's Square Terminal and emails the family an itemised receipt.

**Collect payment** appears on studio party bookings for studio iPads and super-admins. Mobile parties aren't charged
this way.

## What's charged

| Included                                                   | Not included                                               |
| ---------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| The party price per child, for its length and food package | Cakes, take-home bags and products, paid in the Party Form |
| One of each food addition                                  | Party packs                                                |

A party is charged for at least 12 children, even if fewer came. Prices come from Square, so the checkout always
charges the studio's current price.

## Charge a party

<Steps>
  <ol>
    <li>
      <h3>Open the booking and select Collect payment</h3>
      <p>The checkout opens full screen, with an order summary beside each step for the family to follow.</p>
    </li>
    <li>
      <h3>Confirm the party</h3>
      <p>
        Check the party length and food package, and enter the number of children who came. It starts from the booking.
        Fewer than 12 children are charged as 12.
      </p>
    </li>
    <li>
      <h3>Confirm the food additions</h3>
      <p>These start from what the parent ordered in their Party Form. Add or remove any that changed on the day.</p>
    </li>
    <li>
      <h3>Review and charge</h3>
      <p>
        The order summary now shows Square's price, which is what's charged. Add a gift card or a discount if needed,
        then select <strong>Charge</strong>.
      </p>
    </li>
    <li>
      <h3>The family pays on the terminal</h3>
      <p>
        They tap, insert or swipe their card on the studio's terminal. Keep the checkout open until it shows
        <strong>Payment received</strong>.
      </p>
    </li>
  </ol>
</Steps>

## Gift cards and discounts

Both are optional and tucked behind links on the review step, since most parties don't need them.

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Gift card" icon="approve-check-circle">
    <p>
      Enter the card number with <strong>Use a gift card</strong>. Its balance comes off first and the terminal charges
      the rest. If the card covers everything, the button becomes <strong>Pay with gift card</strong>.
    </p>
  </Card>
  <Card title="Discount" icon="pencil">
    <p>
      Enter a dollar amount and a reason with <strong>Add a discount</strong>. The reason is kept for us and isn't shown
      to the family; their receipt just says "Discount". A discount can cover the whole party.
    </p>
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## After it's paid

- The family is emailed an itemised receipt, with a link to Square's receipt.
- The booking shows **Paid**. Its **Paid** amount links to the Square receipt.
- The party's Zoho deal records the amount paid, with a note of what was charged.
- A paid party can't be charged again.

## If the charge doesn't go through

The checkout says why: the family cancelled on the terminal, staff cancelled it, or the terminal timed out before they
paid. Nothing was charged. Select **Send to terminal again** to retry, or **Edit the charge** to change something first.

To stop a charge while it's on the terminal, select **Cancel charge**. If the family had already paid, the checkout
shows the payment instead.

Closing the checkout or reloading the iPad doesn't lose a charge. Open **Collect payment** again and it picks up where it
was.

## Troubleshooting

| What you see                                                              | What to do                                                                                                            |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| No **Collect payment** button                                             | It only shows on unpaid studio parties, for studio iPads and super-admins. Check you're signed in on the studio iPad. |
| A gift card is inactive, has no balance or could not be charged           | Check the number with the family, or remove the gift card and charge their card on the terminal.                      |
| "This party's price isn't sold at this studio"                            | The party's price isn't set up for the studio in Square. Record the booking and report it.                            |
| The checkout couldn't check the payment and says it may have gone through | Don't charge again. Reopen the booking: if it shows **Paid**, you're done. If not, record the booking and report it.  |

The studio's terminal is set up once for all Portal payments. See [Studio Square Terminal](/portal/studio-terminal/).
