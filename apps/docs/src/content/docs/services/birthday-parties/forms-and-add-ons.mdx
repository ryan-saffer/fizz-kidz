title: Party forms and add-ons
description: How the Party Form and cake form collect final details, take payment for cakes and goodies, and notify the studio and suppliers.
---

Fizz Kidz uses one party form in two modes. The **Party Form** gathers final party details. The **cake form** is an
earlier way to order a cake and take-home goodies.

## Which link to send

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Party Form" icon="open-book">
    <p>
      Use for final guest numbers, creations, food package, additions, fun facts, questions and optional paid items.
    </p>
  </Card>
  <Card title="Cake form" icon="add-document">
    <p>
      Use for a cake, take-home bag or kit order before the Party Form is due. Cakes are available only for studio
      parties outside Geelong and Werribee; families there can still order goodies.
    </p>
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

Both links are tied to the booking. The form opens with the booking's parent and child details and shows anything the
family has already ordered.

<Aside type="caution" title="Never reuse a link for another family">
  The link decides which booking is updated. Always copy the form link from the correct booking menu.
</Aside>

## Automatic sends

| Workflow               | Who it selects                                     | Who it skips                                                                           |
| ---------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Early cake/order email | Eligible parties roughly 3-4 weeks away            | Mobile, Geelong, Werribee, or a booking already recording a cake, take-home bag or kit |
| Party Form email       | Parties in the following Tuesday-to-Tuesday window | No `Party Form filled in` filter on the initial send                                   |
| Party Form reminder    | Parties in the upcoming Tuesday-to-Tuesday window  | Any booking already marked `Party Form filled in`                                      |

The exact send time is scheduled automatically. If a customer needs the form now, use **Copy form link > Party form**
from the booking menu instead of waiting for the scheduled email.

## Paid now and paid later

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Paid in the form" icon="approve-check-circle">
    <p>
      Ice-cream cakes, take-home bags and kits are paid on the form's review page by card, Apple Pay or Google Pay.
      Discount codes and Square gift cards can be used. Nothing needs paying if none of these are ordered.
    </p>
  </Card>
  <Card title="Paid at the party" icon="warning">
    <p>
      Food additions and the party itself are saved on the booking and charged at the party with
      <a href="/services/birthday-parties/collect-payment/">Collect payment</a>. The Portal labels the additions
      <strong>Pay at party</strong>.
    </p>
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

Take-home bags and kits are ordered in lots of at least 12. Once an item is ordered, the family can add any number
more, for example after late RSVPs. Each party can order one cake; once it's ordered, the form shows it instead of
offering another.

## What happens after submission

<Steps>
  <ol>
    <li>
      <h3>The Portal booking is updated</h3>
      <p>
        The Party Form marks <code>Party Form filled in</code>, then saves final numbers, creations, food selection,
        additions, questions and ordered items. The cake form saves only the cake and goodies, and does not mark the
        Party Form complete.
      </p>
    </li>
    <li>
      <h3>The family receives a summary</h3>
      <p>
        The customer receives a Party Form summary, or an order summary from the cake form when something was ordered,
        plus Square's payment receipt for anything paid.
      </p>
    </li>
    <li>
      <h3>The studio and suppliers are told</h3>
      <p>Studio and supplier notices are sent according to the selections below.</p>
    </li>
  </ol>
</Steps>

## Internal notifications

| Trigger                      | Automatic effect                                                           |
| ---------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Party Form submitted again   | Studio inbox receives the old and new details                              |
| Studio food package changed  | Studio inbox receives a separate notice, including on the first form       |
| Customer leaves a question   | Studio inbox receives the question with the parent as reply-to             |
| Take-home bag or kit ordered | Studio inbox is notified                                                   |
| Cake ordered                 | `orders@birthdaycakeshop.com.au` is emailed and the studio is blind-copied |
| Take-home bags ordered       | `orders@birthdaycakeshop.com.au` is emailed                                |

Contact the supplier directly for late changes or corrections to an order already sent.

## Repeated forms need care

- A second Party Form sends a comparison email to the studio.
- New take-home bag and kit quantities are added to quantities already on the booking; they are not treated as a replacement.
- Previously selected unpaid additions may still need manual correction in the Portal if the customer removes them on a later form.
- Guest numbers, creations and food-package values are updated from the latest Party Form.

<Aside type="caution" title="A repeat form can create a new order">
  Ordering more goodies on a later form charges the family again and sends another supplier email. To change a cake
  already ordered, the family emails <code>bookings@fizzkidz.com.au</code>; use the approved correction or refund
  process for an existing paid order.
</Aside>
