Fizz Kidz uses one hosted Paperform in two modes. The **Party Form** gathers final event details. The **cake form** is an
earlier route for eligible cakes, take-home bags and products.

## Which link to send

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Party Form" icon="open-book">
    <p>
      Use for final guest numbers, creations, food package, additions, fun facts, questions and optional paid items.
    </p>
  </Card>
  <Card title="Cake form" icon="add-document">
    <p>
      Use for an eligible customer's cake, take-home bag or product order before the main Party Form is due. Cakes are
      available only for studio parties outside Geelong.
    </p>
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

Both links are tied to the booking. When opened, Fizz Kidz prefills Paperform with the current location, parent, child,
food-package and previous paid-item details.

<Aside type="caution" title="Never reuse a link for another family">
  The booking ID controls which Portal record is updated. Always copy the form link from the correct booking menu.
</Aside>

## Automatic sends

| Workflow               | Who it selects                                     | Who it skips                                                                     |
| ---------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Early cake/order email | Eligible parties roughly 3-4 weeks away            | Mobile, Geelong, or a booking already recording a cake, take-home bag or product |
| Party Form email       | Parties in the following Tuesday-to-Tuesday window | No `Party Form filled in` filter on the initial send                             |
| Party Form reminder    | Parties in the upcoming Tuesday-to-Tuesday window  | Any booking already marked `Party Form filled in`                                |

The exact send time is scheduled automatically. If a customer needs the form now, use **Copy form link > Party form**
from the booking menu instead of waiting for the scheduled email.

## Paid now and paid later

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Paid in Square checkout" icon="approve-check-circle">
    <p>
      Fizz cake, take-home bags and products selected in the form create a Square payment link. Square payment happens
      before the form is completed.
    </p>
  </Card>
  <Card title="Paid at the party" icon="warning">
    <p>
      Food additions and the party itself are saved on the booking and charged at the party with
      <a href="/services/birthday-parties/collect-payment/">Collect payment</a>. The Portal labels the additions
      <strong>Pay at party</strong>. Party packs aren't charged by the form.
    </p>
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

If there are no paid items, the customer skips Square and goes directly to form completion. If paid items are selected,
the order is paid in Square before the form is completed.

## What happens after completion

<Steps>
  <ol>
    <li>
      <h3>Square records paid items</h3>
      <p>
        Cakes, take-home bags and products are paid through the Square checkout. Supplier-fulfilled take-home bags and
        products are added back to the studio's Square inventory.
      </p>
    </li>
    <li>
      <h3>The Portal booking is updated</h3>
      <p>
        The Party Form marks <code>Party Form filled in</code>, then saves final numbers, creations, food selection,
        additions, questions and ordered items. Cake-only mode does not mark the Party Form complete.
      </p>
    </li>
    <li>
      <h3>Customer and internal emails are sent</h3>
      <p>
        The customer receives a Party Form summary, or an order summary for cake mode when something was ordered. Studio
        and supplier notices are sent according to the selections below.
      </p>
    </li>
  </ol>
</Steps>

## Internal notifications

| Trigger                          | Automatic effect                                                           |
| -------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Party Form submitted again       | Studio inbox receives the old and new details                              |
| Studio food package changed      | Studio inbox receives a separate notice, including on the first form       |
| Too many creations selected      | Studio inbox is asked to contact the parent and agree on a workable set    |
| Customer leaves a question       | Studio inbox receives the question with the parent as reply-to             |
| Party pack selected              | Studio inbox is notified; the party pack remains unpaid by this checkout   |
| Take-home bag or product ordered | Studio inbox is notified                                                   |
| Cake ordered                     | `orders@birthdaycakeshop.com.au` is emailed and the studio is blind-copied |
| Take-home bags ordered           | `orders@birthdaycakeshop.com.au` is emailed                                |

Contact the supplier directly for late changes or corrections to an order already sent.

## Repeated forms need care

- A second Party Form sends a comparison email to the studio.
- New take-home bag and product quantities are added to quantities already on the booking; they are not treated as a replacement.
- Previously selected unpaid additions may still need manual correction in the Portal if the customer removes them on a later form.
- A new paid order can create another Square payment and another supplier email.
- Guest numbers, creations and food-package values are updated from the latest Party Form.

<Aside type="caution" title="A repeat form can create a new order">
  Repeating the form can add quantities, charge the family again and send another supplier email. Use the approved
  correction or refund process for an existing paid order instead.
</Aside>
