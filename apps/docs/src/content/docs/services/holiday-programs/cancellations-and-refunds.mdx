---
title: Cancellations and refunds
description: What happens when a Holiday Program appointment is changed or cancelled in Acuity.
---

import { Aside, Badge, Card, CardGrid, LinkCard } from '@astrojs/starlight/components'

Each booking confirmation has a <strong>Change / Cancel</strong> link beside every child/session. It opens that
individual Acuity appointment.

## The 48-hour rule

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="At least 48 hours before" icon="approve-check-circle">
    <Badge text="Automatic" variant="tip" size="small" />
    <br />
    Cancelling the appointment automatically refunds the eligible amount for that child and session.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Inside 48 hours" icon="warning">
    <Badge text="No automatic refund" variant="caution" size="small" />
    <br />
    The appointment is cancelled and a cancellation email is sent, but no refund is created.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Free line" icon="information">
    A fully discounted appointment can be cancelled, but there is no paid amount to return.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Gift card + card" icon="seti:lock">
    A split payment is refunded to the original gift card and card, up to the amount paid with each.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

<Aside type="note" title="Cancellation and rescheduling are different">
  Cancelling applies the 48-hour refund rule. Rescheduling moves the appointment and keeps the booking active; it does
  not create a refund.
</Aside>

<Aside type="tip" title="What the customer receives">
  Every cancellation sends a cancellation email. When a refund is made, the email links to the refund receipt, which
  shows the amount. Each <strong>Change / Cancel</strong> link applies to one child and session.
</Aside>
