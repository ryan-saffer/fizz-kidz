Holiday Program checkout brings together two systems: <strong>Square records the money</strong> and{' '}

<strong>Acuity records the place in each session</strong>.

## Customer journey

<Steps>
  <ol>
    <li>
      <h3>Select a studio and sessions</h3>
      <p>
        The booking form shows Acuity availability. Full sessions cannot be selected, and low availability is shown to
        the customer.
      </p>
    </li>
    <li>
      <h3>Add children and contact details</h3>
      <p>
        The customer supplies parent, emergency contact, allergy, anaphylaxis, asthma and additional-needs information.
        Anaphylactic children need an anaphylaxis plan. If a child requires an asthma action plan, the customer must
        upload it too, even if the child has no allergies. Each plan must be a PDF smaller than 5MB. The total is
        recalculated for every child in every selected session.
      </p>
    </li>
    <li>
      <h3>Review and pay</h3>
      <p>
        A valid discount code is applied to the booking total. A Fizz Kidz gift card is then used first, with any
        balance paid by card, Apple Pay or Google Pay.
      </p>
    </li>
    <li>
      <h3>Receive confirmation</h3>
      <p>
        The customer sees <strong>Booked!</strong> and receives a Fizz Kidz confirmation with each child/session, the
        location, receipt when available, and an individual <strong>Change / Cancel</strong> link to manage that
        appointment in the Portal.
      </p>
    </li>
  </ol>
</Steps>

## Booking outcome

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Current availability" icon="warning">
    Availability is confirmed at checkout. If there are not enough places for every selected child, the customer sees a
    session-full message and can choose another session.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Payment summary" icon="seti:lock">
    The order includes every child and session. Discount codes reduce the total, and gift card and card payments can
    share the balance.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Session bookings" icon="information">
    Each child has a separate appointment for each session, carrying the contact and safety details needed for
    attendance.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Confirmation" icon="email">
    The confirmation lists all booked sessions and their management links. Additional-needs answers are also added to
    the staff follow-up list.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

<Aside type="tip" title="Morning + afternoon">
  Selecting both sessions on the same date marks those appointments as <strong>All Day</strong> for attendance. It is
  still two paid session lines and two Acuity appointments per child.
</Aside>

## Discounts and gift cards

- One discount code can be used at checkout. Codes may be invalid, expired, exhausted or limited to one use per
  customer.
- A fixed discount cannot be greater than the booking total.
- A gift card can cover all or part of the total. Any remainder moves to the customer's other payment method.
- Gift cards are a payment method, not a discount. See [Gift cards](/services/gift-cards/) for how they are sold and
  settled.
