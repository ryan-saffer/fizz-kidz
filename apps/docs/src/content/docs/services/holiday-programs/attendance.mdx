The Holiday Program attendance list is built from <strong>Acuity appointments</strong>. Each row represents one child in
one session, not a whole Square order.

## What staff see

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Child details" icon="open-book">
    Name and age, plus parent and emergency contact details from checkout.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Safety details" icon="warning">
    <Badge text="Allergy" variant="caution" size="small" /> <Badge text="Anaphylactic" variant="caution" size="small" />{' '}
    <Badge text="Asthma" variant="caution" size="small" />
    <br />
    Allergy details, anaphylaxis plans and asthma action plans are available from the child's row.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Booking context" icon="information">
    <Badge text="All Day" variant="note" size="small" /> <Badge text="Includes Notes" variant="default" size="small" />
    <br />
    <strong>All Day</strong> appears when the child has morning and afternoon sessions on the same date.{' '}
    <strong>Includes Notes</strong> highlights additional information.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Attendance state" icon="approve-check-circle">
    A row with no attendance label is waiting to be signed in. Signing in applies Acuity's <strong>Checked in</strong>{' '}
    label; <strong>Undo Sign In</strong> removes it.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

<Aside type="caution" title="Review medical plans first">
  When a child needs an anaphylaxis plan or asthma action plan, the Portal asks you to open and verify it before
  completing sign-in. If the child needs both plans, verify the first, then review the next before signing in.
</Aside>

## Sign-in flow

<Steps>
  <ol>
    <li>
      <h3>Open the correct class</h3>
      <p>Confirm the studio, date and session time before using the list.</p>
    </li>
    <li>
      <h3>Expand the child's row</h3>
      <p>
        Review allergies, notes, parent details and emergency contact information. For all-day children, remember that
        the morning and afternoon remain separate appointments.
      </p>
    </li>
    <li>
      <h3>Sign in</h3>
      <p>
        The Portal saves the <strong>Checked in</strong> label back to Acuity. Holiday Program check-in also updates the
        customer follow-up record when it matches their first booked program date.
      </p>
    </li>
    <li>
      <h3>Correct mistakes</h3>
      <p>
        Use <strong>Undo Sign In</strong> to remove the attendance label.
      </p>
    </li>
  </ol>
</Steps>
