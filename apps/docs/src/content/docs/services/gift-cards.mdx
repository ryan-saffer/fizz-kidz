Fizz Kidz gift cards are managed by Square. The current sales channel is the shared Square e-gift card website, while
redemptions can happen in supported online checkouts or at the studio through the Portal.

<Aside type="danger" title="Sell through the gift-card website only">
  Do not create or sell a new gift card at a studio. Shared gift-card sales are routed through the Fizz Kidz HQ Square
  setup so a card can later be redeemed at a different studio without the sale being credited to the wrong franchisee.
</Aside>

## What customers can do

- Buy an e-gift card for themselves or another person.
- Schedule delivery by email or text using Square's checkout options.
- Create a group gift and invite other contributors.
- Reload an existing Square gift card.
- Check the balance from the same Square page.

<Aside type="note" title="Physical cards: accept, but do not promise new sales">
  Fizz Kidz currently sells e-gift cards through the website. The Portal's supported booking checkouts accept the number
  from a Fizz Kidz physical or e-gift card, so an existing compatible physical card can still be redeemed. Do not tell a
  customer that a studio can issue a new physical card unless HQ has introduced and communicated that process.
</Aside>

## How online redemption works

<Steps>
  <ol>
    <li>
      <h3>Customer applies the gift card</h3>
      <p>The Portal checks the gift-card number and AUD balance with Square. A non-AUD card is not supported.</p>
    </li>
    <li>
      <h3>The Portal shows the amount to apply</h3>
      <p>
        The checkout applies up to the order total. If the gift-card balance is smaller than the total, the Portal shows
        the remaining amount for card or wallet payment. If it is larger, the unused balance remains on the gift card.
      </p>
    </li>
    <li>
      <h3>Square processes the payment</h3>
      <p>
        The gift card must be <strong>ACTIVE</strong>. Square applies the gift-card amount and charges any remainder to
        the customer's card or wallet as one order.
      </p>
    </li>
  </ol>
</Steps>

<Aside type="note" title="Buying a gift card does not make a booking">
  A gift-card purchase adds value to the card. The customer still needs to complete the normal service booking.
</Aside>

## Refunds and cancellations

Eligible Holiday Program, Play Lab and current Preschool Program cancellations are refunded to the original payment
methods. This means a split gift-card/card purchase can also have a split refund.

<Aside type="caution" title="Refunds return to the original payment methods">
  Refund eligibility depends on the service's cancellation rules. The automated program workflows generally do not
  refund cancellations inside 48 hours, and Play Lab term enrolments have separate handling. A split gift-card/card
  payment may also have a split refund, so do not promise that the full refund will appear on the bank card.
</Aside>

For an in-studio manual refund, refund the original Square transaction using the approved Square process. Do not replace
it with an off-system cash adjustment.

## Franchisee funds

The current documented operating arrangement is:

1. Shared gift-card sales settle to the dedicated Fizz Kidz HQ Square location.
2. Redemptions are recorded at the studio location processing the service or POS sale.
3. HQ reviews Square's redemption reporting by location at month end.
4. HQ reimburses each studio for value redeemed, less the applicable Square fees under the current arrangement.

<Aside type="note" title="Settlement is an HQ process">
  The Portal records redemptions at the service location, but it does not calculate or pay the monthly franchisee
  settlement. HQ uses Square redemption reporting to calculate each studio's remittance.
</Aside>

## Troubleshooting

### Gift card not found

1. Ask the customer to read the gift-card number again, including all digits.
2. Remove spaces and hyphens; the Portal normally does this automatically.
3. Make sure they have not supplied the Square purchase order number, receipt number or delivery email instead.
4. Use the official Square balance-check page.
5. If Square cannot find it, contact HQ with the purchase details and only the last four digits. Never send the full
   number in an unsecured message.

### Gift card is inactive

Square can report a card as deactivated, blocked, pending or unknown. Only an active card can be redeemed. Check the card
in Square or contact HQ when the customer needs help with its status.

### Balance is zero or lower than expected

- Check Square's balance and activity rather than relying on the original purchase amount.
- Confirm whether part of the card was already used in another booking or studio.

### Studio settlement looks wrong

Compare the HQ remittance against Square's location redemption report, not gift-card sales at the studio. Include the
reporting month, studio and disputed redeemed amount when contacting HQ.

## Quick team checklist

- Sell new cards only through the official Square e-gift card link.
- Redeem online where the checkout displays a gift-card field, and at the studio with Collect payment or Sell products.
- Share only the last four gift-card digits when asking for support.
