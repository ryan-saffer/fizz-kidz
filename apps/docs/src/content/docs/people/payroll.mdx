The Portal turns published Sling shifts into a CSV for UpSheets. The CSV is reviewed and imported into Xero manually; generating it does not create a pay run.

<Aside type="caution" title="Sling must be correct first">
  Payroll uses published Sling shifts, including their employee, location, position, dates, times, and notes. Finalise
  the roster before generating the file.
</Aside>

## Generate the CSV

1. Finalise and publish all casual employee shifts in Sling.
2. In the Portal, open **Payroll**.
3. Select the payroll period. The range must be 28 days or less.
4. Select **Generate Timesheets**.
5. Review the result sections and download the CSV.
6. Upload the reviewed file to [UpSheets](https://app.upsheets.com/login).
7. Import from UpSheets into Xero, create the pay run, and complete any adjustments identified during review.

## What the Portal calculates

For each published shift in the selected business and date range, the Portal:

- matches the Sling employee to Xero by email;
- maps the Sling position and location to a Xero pay item and tracking activity;
- separates overtime after 10 hours in one shift or 38 hours in a week;
- applies the special handling for on-call, Sunday, and higher-rate shift variants;
- adds eligible laundry allowance rows and enforces the weekly cap;
- flags birthday rate changes, under-18 super checks, and short shifts; and
- carries Sling shift notes into the CSV for review.

Uploading to UpSheets, creating the Xero pay run, and adding reimbursements from notes remain manual payroll tasks.

## Review every exception

### Skipped employees

Skipped employees are listed in the result and are not included in the CSV.

Add the employee to the correct Xero organisation with an ordinary-hours rate, and use the same email as Sling. Then generate the CSV again and include any required backpay.

### Birthdays in the period

The Portal lists casual employees whose birthday falls in the selected range, including whether they worked.

- Decide whether their rate changes because of the birthday.
- If it changes, update the Xero pay template.
- Remove pre-birthday rows from the CSV before import, then add those hours to the pay run at the previous rate.
- Pay particular attention when the employee turns 18 because the pay items may also change.

### Under 18 and more than 30 hours

Employees under 18 who worked more than 30 hours in a week require superannuation in the pay run.

### Shifts below the minimum length

The Portal lists published shifts shorter than three hours, or shorter than four hours on Sunday. Correct the roster or use the notes to explain the short shift.

### Overtime

The Portal separates overtime after 10 hours in one shift or 38 hours in a week. On-call hours do not accrue overtime. Sunday hours remain on the Sunday pay item when that rate is higher.

### Allowances and reimbursements

Laundry allowances are generated automatically for eligible uniformed shifts. Other items, such as travel or kilometre reimbursements, are not inferred from the roster. Review the CSV **notes** column and add required adjustments in Xero.
