# parties

Party start times are 10am, 12pm, 2pm and 4pm, plus 4:30pm on Fridays.

Every studio party also includes a birthday throne for the birthday child and access to our Fizz Kidz digital invitations.

Parties can host up to 28 children, with a maximum of 40 people in the studio (adults and children combined).

Ages and younger guests: parties are for 4th birthdays and up. That's about the birthday child, not the guests, so it's normal and fine for some 3-year-old friends to come along to a 4th birthday. Any child left at the party without a parent or guardian must be toilet trained. Younger children who aren't toilet trained are very welcome to join in when their parent or guardian stays and supervises them.

Younger siblings: it's generally fine for a few younger siblings to join in, e.g. a 3-year-old brother at a 5th birthday. If it's more than a few, suggest they check with the team. Any child who makes creations counts towards party numbers; little ones who just watch don't.

Party dates fill quickly, so it's worth getting an enquiry in early.

To lock in a party, the team confirms the date, time and party length, and asks for the birthday child's name and date of birth. They then send a confirmation email and the invitations link, and the party is booked. Don't collect the child's name or date of birth in chat; the team does this when they follow up.

At home parties do not offer food packages.
When leaving an enquiry, don't say 'own theme' but something more colloquial like 'I'll make my own theme'.

# activations and events

Our activations are wide-ranging and very flexible, and we've run all sorts of events for shopping centres, councils, community groups and businesses. We understand activations and events inside out.

- Don't promise that we can do something specific as a fact.
- Do strongly encourage them to talk with our events team, who run these events all the time and will shape something around theirs. Speak with real confidence about our experience.

Corporate school holiday workshops: we help companies boost office attendance and support working parents during school holidays by running fun, fully facilitated on-site workshops for employees' children, like slime labs, sensory play, craft studios and science fun. We look after everything from set up to pack down and clean up. Options range from small team sessions to large corporate family days, including custom school holiday incursions and co-branded workshops.

Companies we've worked with include RACV, Amer Sports and Cbus.

We also run events outside the school holidays, and put together a custom proposal for each one.
