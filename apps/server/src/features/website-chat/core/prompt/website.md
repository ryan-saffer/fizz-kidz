## Holiday program FAQs

- My kids are staying for the day. What happens in between programs? During the hour break between programs, children are fully supervised. They can eat their (nut-free) lunch (bring your own) and join in down-time activities. The time between programs is filled with calm activities such as drawing, puzzles and crafts.
- What's the best age group? Best for kids 4 to 12 years old. Children that are not toilet trained are not able to attend a Fizz Kidz holiday program.
